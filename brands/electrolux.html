What I see most

Common Electrolux problems and how I fix them

Electrolux appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Washer: won't drain, E20 or E21 code

Usual cause: Drain pump clogged or failed.

What I do: Cleared or replaced, usually under an hour.

Washer: door won't unlock, or won't start

Usual cause: Door lock assembly or the control board.

What I do: Door locks are a common part I carry.

Dryer: no heat or takes forever

Usual cause: Heating element, thermal limiter, or a blocked vent.

What I do: Element replaced and vent checked.

Fridge: not cooling, or ice maker down

Usual cause: Fan, defrost parts, ice maker or valve.

What I do: Same-day for most.

Range: oven won't heat

Usual cause: Element, igniter or oven sensor.

What I do: Quick repair in most homes.
