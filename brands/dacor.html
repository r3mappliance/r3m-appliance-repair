What I see most

Common Dacor problems and how I fix them

Dacor appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Oven: won't heat, or takes too long

Usual cause: Igniter or element, oven sensor, or relay board on older units.

What I do: Igniters and sensors are same-visit once the part is in hand.

Cooktop: burner won't light

Usual cause: Igniter, spark module or the burner switch.

What I do: Usually a quick repair.

Built-in fridge: warm or noisy

Usual cause: Condenser coils and fan, defrost system.

What I do: Cleaned and repaired on site.

Wall oven: error code or door lock

Usual cause: Control board or the lock motor.

What I do: Tested before ordering.
