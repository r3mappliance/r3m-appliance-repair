Step 1

What usually causes OE

Most common first. In my experience the first one or two on this list account for most LG OE calls.

  1. 1. Clogged drain pump filter
  2. 2. Kinked or clogged drain hose
  3. 3. Blocked standpipe or sink drain
  4. 4. Excess suds from too much detergent
  5. 5. Failed drain pump
Step 2

What to try before you call

Safe things a homeowner can do. Unplug the washer before reaching into anything.

  1. Check the drain hose for kinks and confirm it is not pushed too far into the standpipe.
  2. Run a Spin Only cycle and listen. A humming pump usually means a clogged filter.
  3. Unplug the washer, open the lower access panel, drain the small hose into a pan and clean the drain pump filter.
  4. Run a Tub Clean cycle and cut back on detergent.
Step 3

When to stop and call a technician

The filter and hose are clear and the pump is silent, or the washer still will not drain. At that point the fix is usually a part, and guessing at parts gets expensive fast. I test first, then quote, so you only pay for what is actually broken.

Questions

LG OE questions

How do I clear the OE code on my LG washer?

Fix the cause first, then unplug the washer (or turn it off at the breaker) for about a minute and restart. The code clears once the washer no longer sees the problem. If it comes right back, the cause is still there.

Is a LG washer with a OE error worth repairing?

Usually yes. Drain pump and similar parts cost a fraction of a new washer. I tell you the price before any work starts, and if a repair is not worth it I say so.
