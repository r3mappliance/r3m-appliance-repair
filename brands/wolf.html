What I see most

Common Wolf problems and how I fix them

Wolf appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Burner clicks but won't light, or keeps clicking

Usual cause: Dirty or failed spark igniter, or a spark module. Moisture after cleaning is a common cause.

What I do: Igniters cleaned or replaced. The clicking-forever complaint is usually a quick fix.

Oven won't heat, or takes too long

Usual cause: Oven igniter on gas ovens, bake or broil element on electric, or the temperature sensor.

What I do: Igniters and elements are common and quoted before replacement.

Oven temperature off, or error on display

Usual cause: Oven sensor or control board.

What I do: Diagnosed with a real thermometer test, not a guess.

Convection fan loud or not running

Usual cause: Convection fan motor.

What I do: Ordered and replaced within a few days.
