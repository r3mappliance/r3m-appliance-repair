Symptoms

What your dryer is telling you

The problems I see most, what usually causes them, and how I fix them.

No heat, drum still turns

Usual cause: Heating element or thermal fuse on electric dryers, igniter or gas valve coils on gas dryers.

What I do: I carry the common elements, fuses and igniters.

Takes two cycles to dry

Usual cause: Clogged vent line or a lint-packed blower housing. Sometimes a weak heating element.

What I do: I check airflow first. A blocked vent is also a fire risk, so this one matters.

Drum will not turn

Usual cause: Broken belt, worn drum rollers or a failed motor.

What I do: Belts and rollers are quick. Motors are still usually worth it.

Squealing or thumping

Usual cause: Drum rollers, idler pulley or glides.

What I do: Same-day on most dryers.

Shuts off early or overheats

Usual cause: Cycling thermostat, moisture sensor or restricted airflow.

What I do: Diagnosed and replaced on the spot.

Questions

Dryer repair questions

Do you work on gas dryers?

Yes. Igniters, gas valve coils, flame sensors and thermal fuses.

Can you clean the dryer vent too?

I clear the vent at the machine and check airflow through the wall. Full duct cleaning through the roof is a separate service I can point you to.

My dryer smells like burning. Should I keep using it?

No. Unplug it and call. It is usually lint on the element, but it should be looked at before the next load.
