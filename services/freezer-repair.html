Symptoms

What your freezer is telling you

The problems I see most, what usually causes them, and how I fix them.

Freezer not cold enough

Usual cause: Dirty condenser coils, a failed evaporator fan or a defrost problem.

What I do: Same-day in most cases.

Frost building up

Usual cause: Failed defrost heater, thermostat or a door gasket that is not sealing.

What I do: Defrost parts and gaskets replaced on site.

Running constantly

Usual cause: Coils, fan or a thermostat.

What I do: Cleaned and repaired same visit.

Not running at all

Usual cause: Start relay, thermostat or compressor.

What I do: Relays are quick. Compressors I price honestly.

How I work

Freezer repair questions

Do you repair garage freezers?

Yes. Garage units work harder in a Texas summer and fail more often. That is a normal call for me.

How long will the food stay frozen?

A full freezer kept closed holds for about 48 hours, a half-full one about 24. Call early.
