What I see most

Common Sub-Zero problems and how I fix them

Sub-Zero appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Warm, or 'Service' or 'Vacuum Condenser' light on

Usual cause: Condenser coils behind the top grille packed with dust. The unit overheats and struggles.

What I do: I clean the condenser and check the fan on every Sub-Zero call. Most 'warm' complaints end there.

Frost in the freezer, or ice on the back wall

Usual cause: Defrost heater or thermostat, or a worn door gasket.

What I do: Gaskets and defrost parts are ordered and installed within a few days.

Ice maker not producing

Usual cause: Water valve, ice maker assembly or a frozen fill tube.

What I do: Diagnosed and quoted on site.

Temperature swings or error code on display

Usual cause: Thermistor, fan motor or control board.

What I do: Boards are tested, not guessed, before ordering.
