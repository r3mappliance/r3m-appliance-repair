What I see most

Common LG problems and how I fix them

LG appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Fridge: not cooling, compressor running warm

Usual cause: The linear compressor is the known weak point on many LG refrigerators. Sometimes it is only the start relay or the main board.

What I do: I test the compressor and the electronics before quoting. If it really is the compressor, I give you the sealed-system price and a straight opinion on repair versus replace.

Fridge: warm fridge, cold freezer

Usual cause: Evaporator fan or the defrost system. Ice builds on the coil and air stops moving.

What I do: Same-day fix in most cases.

Washer: OE, UE, LE or dE code

Usual cause: OE is drain, UE is balance or suspension, LE is the motor or rotor sensor, dE is the door lock.

What I do: Pumps, door locks and suspension rods are common parts I carry. LE codes get a proper motor test, not a guess.

Washer: loud during spin

Usual cause: Worn bearings on front-loaders, or suspension rods on top-loaders.

What I do: Suspension is a same-day job. Bearings on an older machine are where I will tell you the honest number.

Dryer: no heat or won't start

Usual cause: Heating element, thermal fuse, or the door switch and start relay.

What I do: Common parts on the truck.
