Symptoms

What your ice maker is telling you

The problems I see most, what usually causes them, and how I fix them.

No ice at all

Usual cause: Water inlet valve, frozen fill tube or a failed ice maker assembly.

What I do: All three are same-day repairs.

Small or hollow cubes

Usual cause: Low water pressure, a partially clogged filter or a weak inlet valve.

What I do: Filter and valve checked, replaced if needed.

Ice tastes or smells bad

Usual cause: Old filter or a dirty bin.

What I do: Filter change and cleaning.

Ice maker leaking or overflowing

Usual cause: Stuck inlet valve or a bad level sensor.

What I do: Replaced on the spot.

Dispenser not working

Usual cause: Auger motor, dispenser switch or a frozen chute.

What I do: Diagnosed and repaired same visit.

How I work

Diagnose first. Price before parts.

I check water supply and pressure first, then the valve, fill tube and the ice maker itself. Most units are back to making ice within a day.

Repair or replace?

An ice maker assembly or valve is always worth replacing. If the refrigerator itself is on its way out, I will tell you before you spend the money.
