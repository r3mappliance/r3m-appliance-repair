What I see most

Common KitchenAid problems and how I fix them

KitchenAid appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Dishwasher: won't drain, or clean light blinking

Usual cause: Drain pump, check valve, or a control/heater fault behind the blinking light.

What I do: Pumps are on the truck. The blink code tells me where to look.

Dishwasher: not cleaning well

Usual cause: Circulation pump, wash arm clogged, or water not getting hot.

What I do: Cleaned, tested and repaired the same visit.

Fridge: warm or ice maker stopped

Usual cause: Evaporator fan, defrost parts, ice maker or valve.

What I do: Common parts I carry.

Range: oven won't heat or F code

Usual cause: Bake element or igniter, oven sensor, or control board.

What I do: Elements and igniters same-day.

Microwave: runs, no heat

Usual cause: Magnetron, diode or door switches.

What I do: Repaired in place.
