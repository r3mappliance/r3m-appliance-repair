Symptoms

What your oven & range is telling you

The problems I see most, what usually causes them, and how I fix them.

Oven not heating

Usual cause: Bake element on electric, igniter on gas. Both are the most common oven repair there is.

What I do: I carry common elements and igniters on the truck.

Uneven baking or wrong temperature

Usual cause: Failed temperature sensor or a weak igniter that is slow to open the gas valve.

What I do: Sensor swap and calibration, same visit.

Burner clicking or not lighting

Usual cause: Dirty or failed spark igniter, or a spark module.

What I do: Cleaned or replaced on the spot.

Door will not close or seal

Usual cause: Worn door gasket or broken hinge.

What I do: Gaskets and hinges replaced same day.

Error code or dead display

Usual cause: Control board or a tripped thermal fuse after a self-clean cycle.

What I do: I confirm before ordering a board.

How I work

Diagnose first. Price before parts.

I test the element or igniter, the sensor and the controls. Most oven repairs are one part and under an hour. Before Thanksgiving, do not wait until the week of.

Repair or replace?

Elements, igniters, sensors and gaskets are always worth fixing. A control board on an older budget range is the one case where I will talk through replacing instead.
