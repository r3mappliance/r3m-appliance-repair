What I see most

Common Monogram problems and how I fix them

Monogram appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Built-in fridge: warm, or running loud

Usual cause: Condenser coils up top packed with dust, condenser fan, or the defrost system.

What I do: Coil cleaning on a built-in is a maintenance item most owners skip. I do it on every service call.

Column freezer: frost build-up

Usual cause: Door gasket or the defrost system.

What I do: Gasket and defrost parts ordered and replaced, usually within a few days.

Professional range: oven won't light or heats unevenly

Usual cause: Oven igniter, gas valve or the temperature sensor.

What I do: Igniters are common and repairable.

Wine reserve: not holding temperature

Usual cause: Fan, thermistor or the cooling system.

What I do: Diagnosed on site with a clear price before parts.
