What I see most

Common GE Profile problems and how I fix them

GE Profile appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Fridge: not cooling, display working

Usual cause: Fan, defrost system or inverter/control board.

What I do: Diagnosed on site, boards confirmed before they are ordered.

Washer: leaks or vibrates hard

Usual cause: Door boot or drain hose on front-loaders, worn shocks for the vibration.

What I do: Both are same-day repairs.

Dishwasher: won't drain or leaves dishes wet

Usual cause: Drain pump, check valve, or the vent and heater circuit.

What I do: Common parts I carry.

Induction or electric cooktop: burner dead or error code

Usual cause: Failed element, generator board on induction, or a touch control problem.

What I do: Element swaps are quick. Induction boards are ordered after testing.

Range: oven temperature off or not heating

Usual cause: Oven sensor, element, or relay board.

What I do: Same-day for sensors and elements.
