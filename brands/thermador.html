What I see most

Common Thermador problems and how I fix them

Thermador appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Star burner won't light or clicks

Usual cause: Igniter or spark module. Moisture in the burner after cleaning is common.

What I do: Dried, cleaned or replaced. Quick on most models.

Oven won't heat, or temperature off

Usual cause: Oven igniter or element, temperature sensor, or relay board.

What I do: Tested before any part is ordered.

Wall oven: door lock stuck or error code

Usual cause: Door lock motor or control.

What I do: Ordered and replaced within days.

Built-in fridge column: warm

Usual cause: Condenser and fan, defrost, or gasket.

What I do: Coil cleaning and fans done on site.
