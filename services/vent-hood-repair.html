Symptoms

What your vent hood is telling you

The problems I see most, what usually causes them, and how I fix them.

Fan not running

Usual cause: Failed fan motor, switch or control board.

What I do: Motors and switches replaced on site.

Weak suction

Usual cause: Greasy filters, a blocked duct or a stuck damper.

What I do: Cleaned and cleared.

Lights out

Usual cause: Bulbs, sockets or a transformer on LED units.

What I do: Quick fix.

Rattling or loud

Usual cause: Loose blower wheel or a failing motor bearing.

What I do: Tightened or replaced.

Downdraft will not rise

Usual cause: Lift motor or switch.

What I do: Diagnosed and repaired.

How I work

Diagnose first. Price before parts.

I check filters and the damper first, since that is the cause of most weak-suction complaints, then the motor and switches.

Repair or replace?

Motors, switches and lights are worth fixing. On a builder-grade hood past fifteen years, replacing can be the better value and I will say so.
