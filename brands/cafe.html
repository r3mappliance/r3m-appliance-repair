What I see most

Common Café problems and how I fix them

Café appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Fridge: warm, or ice and water stopped

Usual cause: Fan, defrost parts, water valve or the dispenser control.

What I do: Same-day in most cases.

Range: oven won't hold temperature, or convection fan noisy

Usual cause: Oven sensor, element, or the convection fan motor.

What I do: Diagnosed and quoted before parts are ordered.

Dishwasher: won't start or won't drain

Usual cause: Door latch, control, or drain pump.

What I do: Common parts on the truck.

Gas cooktop: burner won't light

Usual cause: Spark module or a dirty or failed igniter.

What I do: Usually under an hour.
