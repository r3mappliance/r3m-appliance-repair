Symptoms

What your garbage disposal is telling you

The problems I see most, what usually causes them, and how I fix them.

Hums but does not grind

Usual cause: Jammed flywheel, usually a bone, pit or utensil.

What I do: Cleared and reset. Quick.

Dead, no sound

Usual cause: Tripped reset button, failed switch or a burned-out motor.

What I do: Reset or replaced.

Leaking underneath

Usual cause: Failed seal, cracked housing or loose sink flange.

What I do: Seals and flanges replaced; a cracked housing means a new unit.

Drains slowly or backs up

Usual cause: Clogged drain line or a partially jammed unit.

What I do: Cleared on site.

How I work

Diagnose first. Price before parts.

I check the jam and reset first, then the electrical and the seals. If the unit is done, I can replace it the same day.

Repair or replace?

Jams and switches are worth fixing. A leaking housing or seized motor on an older unit means replacement, and a new disposal is inexpensive.
