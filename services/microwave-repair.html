Symptoms

What your microwave is telling you

The problems I see most, what usually causes them, and how I fix them.

Runs but does not heat

Usual cause: Magnetron, high-voltage diode or capacitor.

What I do: Magnetrons on built-ins are usually worth replacing.

Sparking inside

Usual cause: Damaged waveguide cover or chipped interior paint.

What I do: Cover replaced, cavity checked.

Turntable or light not working

Usual cause: Turntable motor or a bulb.

What I do: Quick fixes.

Dead, no display

Usual cause: Door switch, fuse or control board.

What I do: Switches and fuses are common and quick.

Door will not latch

Usual cause: Broken door hook or switch.

What I do: Replaced same day.
