Symptoms

What your dishwasher is telling you

The problems I see most, what usually causes them, and how I fix them.

Not draining

Usual cause: Clogged filter, failed drain pump or a kinked drain hose. On Bosch units the drain pump is the usual suspect.

What I do: Pump or filter, done on site.

Not cleaning well or leaving film

Usual cause: Weak wash motor, clogged spray arms or a failed heating element that leaves water too cool.

What I do: I test water temperature and pressure and replace what failed.

Leaking from the door

Usual cause: Worn door gasket or a spray arm hitting the door.

What I do: Gasket replaced same day.

Will not start or stops mid-cycle

Usual cause: Door latch, control board or thermal fuse.

What I do: Latches are quick. Boards I price out before ordering.

Loud grinding

Usual cause: Broken glass or a bone in the pump.

What I do: Cleared and checked for damage.

How I work

Diagnose first. Price before parts.

I check drainage, water temperature and the latch first because that covers most complaints. You get a price before any work starts.

Repair or replace?

Dishwashers are worth repairing when the problem is a pump, latch, gasket or element. A leaking tub or a control board on a budget unit past ten years is when I will suggest replacing.
