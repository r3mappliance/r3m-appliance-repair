What I see most

Common Samsung problems and how I fix them

Samsung appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Fridge: ice maker frozen over or not making ice

Usual cause: Frost builds inside the ice room and the fill tube ices up. Very common on Samsung French-door models.

What I do: I defrost the ice room properly, check the seals and drain, and replace the ice maker or auger motor if it has failed. I tell you up front if the design will keep giving trouble.

Fridge: frost on the back panel, fridge section warm

Usual cause: Defrost heater, defrost sensor or a failed evaporator fan behind the Twin Cooling panel.

What I do: Panel off, frost cleared, failed part replaced. Same-day when the part is on the truck.

Washer: stops with a code (nD, 4C, dC, UE)

Usual cause: nD is a drain problem, 4C is water supply, dC is the door lock, UE is an unbalanced load or worn suspension.

What I do: I confirm the code with a test before replacing anything. Drain pumps, door locks and suspension rods are common parts I carry.

Dryer: no heat or heating element keeps failing

Usual cause: Heating element, thermal fuse or a high-limit thermostat, often caused by a restricted vent.

What I do: I replace the element and check airflow so the new one lasts.

Range: oven not heating, or display error

Usual cause: Bake element, igniter on gas models, or the oven sensor. Some models throw errors from the control board.

What I do: Elements and igniters are quick. Boards are diagnosed before they are ordered, never guessed.

Samsung repair questions

Do you fix Samsung ice makers or just replace them?

Both. If the ice maker itself failed, I replace it with a current-revision part. If it is only frozen over, I clear it correctly and fix why it froze, which is cheaper.

My Samsung fridge is only 4 years old. Is it worth repairing?

Almost always, yes. Fans, defrost parts and ice makers are a fraction of the price of a new unit.

Do you work on Samsung Bespoke refrigerators?

Yes. Same cooling system underneath, just a different door design.
