What I see most

Common GE problems and how I fix them

GE appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Fridge: not cooling or warm on the fresh-food side

Usual cause: Evaporator fan, defrost heater or the main control board. GE boards are a known failure on some side-by-side models.

What I do: I test the board rather than guess. Fans and defrost parts are on the truck.

Fridge: ice maker or water dispenser stopped

Usual cause: Water valve, filter housing, or the dispenser control.

What I do: Same-visit repair in most cases.

Washer: won't spin, lid lock error

Usual cause: Lid lock, drive belt or the mode shifter on newer top-loaders.

What I do: Common parts I carry.

Dryer: no heat or won't start

Usual cause: Heating element, thermal fuse, belt switch or the start switch.

What I do: Quick fix, usually under an hour.

Range or wall oven: not heating, F code on display

Usual cause: Bake element or igniter, oven sensor, or a failing relay board.

What I do: Elements and igniters same-day. Boards are confirmed before ordering.
