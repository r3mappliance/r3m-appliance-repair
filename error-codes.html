Samsung error codes

Washer

4C / 4E

Samsung says 4C (4E on older models) means water is not entering the washer correctly.

Washer

5C / 5E, nD

Samsung says 5C (5E or nD on older models) means water is not draining from the washer at the correct speed, or not draining at all.

Washer

UE / Ub, UB, Ur

Samsung says UE (also shown as Ub or UB) means the load inside the tub is unbalanced.

Washer

dC / dE, dE1

Samsung says dC (dE or dE1 on some models) means the washer does not detect the door as closed and locked.

Dryer

HE / hE, HC, hC, HC4

Samsung says the HE / hE / HC family of codes means the dryer is getting too hot, and almost always the reason is restricted airflow.

Dishwasher

LC / LE

Samsung says LC (LE on some models) means the leak sensor in the base detected moisture where it should not be, even if you cannot see a leak.

LG error codes

Washer

OE

LG says OE means the washer could not drain the water from the wash cycle.

Washer

IE

LG says IE is a water inlet error: the tub did not fill to the right level within the expected time.

Washer

UE / uE

LG says capital UE means the washer stopped because the load is unbalanced and needs you to fix it.

Washer

LE / LE1

LG says LE means the motor is overloaded and locked, so the tub could not spin.

Dryer

d80 / d90, d95

LG says d80, d90 and d95 (Flow Sense) mean airflow is restricted somewhere between the dryer and the outside.

Dishwasher

AE / E1

LG says AE (E1 on some models) means a leak inside the dishwasher has tripped the float switch in the base.

Refrigerator

Er FF / Er rF, Er IF, E FF, E rF

LG says Er FF is a freezer fan error, Er rF is a refrigerator fan error and Er IF is an ice maker fan error.

Whirlpool error codes

Washer

F21 / F02, F9 E1, F9E1

Whirlpool says F21 (F02 on some models) means draining is taking longer than expected.

Washer

F5 E2 / F5E2

Whirlpool and Maytag say F5 E2 means the door (or the lid on a top-loader) is not locking properly.

Washer

F20 / FH, F8 E1, F8E1, LF

Whirlpool says F20 (FH on some models) is a water supply problem.

Bosch error codes

Dishwasher

E24 / E25

Bosch says E24 means the dishwasher is not draining, usually because the filter is blocked.

Dishwasher

E15

Bosch says E15 means the safety switch found water in the base of the dishwasher and the leak protection system shut it down.

Frigidaire error codes

Dishwasher

i30

On Frigidaire and Electrolux dishwashers, i30 means water has collected in the base pan and tripped the leak / flood switch.

GE error codes

Oven / Range

F7 / F2

GE says F7 usually points to the key panel: a stuck or shorted key.
