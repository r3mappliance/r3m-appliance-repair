What I see most

Common Maytag problems and how I fix them

Maytag appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Washer: won't spin or lid lock flashing

Usual cause: Lid lock, shifter/actuator, or the drive belt.

What I do: All common parts I carry.

Washer: won't drain or stops mid-cycle

Usual cause: Drain pump clogged or failed.

What I do: Under an hour in most homes.

Dryer: no heat

Usual cause: Heating element or thermal fuse. Check the vent, it is usually the cause.

What I do: Element replaced and airflow checked.

Dryer: squeal or thump

Usual cause: Drum rollers, idler pulley or belt.

What I do: Same-day.

Fridge: not cooling or ice maker down

Usual cause: Fans, defrost system, ice maker or water valve.

What I do: Common Whirlpool-family parts on the truck.
