What I see most

Common Whirlpool problems and how I fix them

Whirlpool appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Fridge: ice maker stopped

Usual cause: Water inlet valve, frozen fill tube or the ice maker module.

What I do: Common Whirlpool ice makers and valves are on the truck.

Fridge: warm, coils packed with dust

Usual cause: Condenser coils and condenser fan, or the defrost system.

What I do: Coil cleaning and fans are same-day. I show you the coils before and after.

Top-load washer: won't spin or drain, or lid lock light flashing

Usual cause: Lid lock, drain pump, or the shifter/actuator on Cabrio-style washers.

What I do: All three are common parts I carry.

Dryer: no heat

Usual cause: Heating element or thermal fuse on electric, igniter or gas coils on gas.

What I do: Usually under an hour.

Microwave: runs but does not heat

Usual cause: Magnetron, diode or the door switches.

What I do: Built-in and over-the-range units repaired in place. I have done magnetron replacements on built-in Whirlpool wall microwaves.
