What I see most

Common Frigidaire problems and how I fix them

Frigidaire appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Fridge: not cooling, or freezer fine and fridge warm

Usual cause: Evaporator fan, defrost heater or thermostat. Compressor start relays also fail on these.

What I do: Fans, defrost and relays are same-day.

Upright freezer: frost or warming up

Usual cause: Defrost timer or heater, or a door gasket.

What I do: Common parts I carry.

Range: oven won't heat or F10 / F30 code

Usual cause: Bake element, igniter, or the oven temperature sensor. F30 usually points to the sensor.

What I do: Sensors and elements are quick.

Dishwasher: won't start or won't drain

Usual cause: Door latch, drain pump, or control.

What I do: Same-visit repair in most cases.

Washer or dryer: no spin, no heat

Usual cause: Pump, door lock, belt, element or thermal fuse.

What I do: Common parts on the truck.
