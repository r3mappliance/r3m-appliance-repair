Step 1

What usually causes IE

Most common first. In my experience the first one or two on this list account for most LG IE calls.

  1. 1. Supply faucets not fully open, or a hose kinked
  2. 2. Clogged inlet filter screens
  3. 3. Low water pressure
  4. 4. Frozen hoses in cold weather (garage laundry rooms)
  5. 5. Faulty water inlet valve
Step 2

What to try before you call

Safe things a homeowner can do. Unplug the washer before reaching into anything.

  1. Open both hot and cold faucets fully and straighten any kinked hoses.
  2. Turn off the water, remove the inlet hoses and clean the mesh filters with a small brush.
  3. Run the faucet into a bucket to confirm the water flow is strong.
  4. In winter, thaw frozen hoses with warm water before restarting.
Step 3

When to stop and call a technician

The supply is strong and the filters are clean but the washer still does not fill. At that point the fix is usually a part, and guessing at parts gets expensive fast. I test first, then quote, so you only pay for what is actually broken.

Questions

LG IE questions

How do I clear the IE code on my LG washer?

Fix the cause first, then unplug the washer (or turn it off at the breaker) for about a minute and restart. The code clears once the washer no longer sees the problem. If it comes right back, the cause is still there.

Is a LG washer with a IE error worth repairing?

Usually yes. Water inlet valve and similar parts cost a fraction of a new washer. I tell you the price before any work starts, and if a repair is not worth it I say so.
