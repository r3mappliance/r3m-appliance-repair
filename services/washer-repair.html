Symptoms

What your washer is telling you

The problems I see most, what usually causes them, and how I fix them.

Will not drain

Usual cause: Drain pump clogged with a sock or coin, or the pump has failed.

What I do: Pump cleared or replaced. Under an hour.

Will not spin or agitate

Usual cause: Lid switch or door lock on newer machines, drive belt or coupler on older ones.

What I do: All common parts I carry.

Leaking water

Usual cause: Door boot on front-loaders, fill hoses, drain hose or the pump housing.

What I do: Catch it early and it stays a small repair instead of a floor repair.

Error code on the display

Usual cause: Samsung and LG codes usually point to the drain pump, door lock or a sensor.

What I do: I read the code, confirm it with a test, then replace only what failed.

Loud banging or walking

Usual cause: Worn suspension rods or shock absorbers, or the unit is out of level.

What I do: Suspension parts are a same-day job.

How I work

Diagnose first. Price before parts.

I run the machine, pull the code if there is one, and check the pump, door lock and drive parts in that order. You get a price before the repair starts.

Repair or replace?

Pumps, switches, locks, belts and hoses are always worth fixing. A cracked outer tub or a failed bearing on a ten-year-old front-loader is where I will be honest about replacing.
