Symptoms

What your wine cooler is telling you

The problems I see most, what usually causes them, and how I fix them.

Not cooling

Usual cause: Dirty condenser, failed fan or a thermoelectric module on smaller units.

What I do: Cleaned and repaired on site.

Temperature swings

Usual cause: Thermostat or control board.

What I do: Tested and replaced.

Noisy fan

Usual cause: Failed evaporator or condenser fan.

What I do: Fan swap, same day.

Frost or moisture inside

Usual cause: Door gasket or defrost issue.

What I do: Gasket replaced.

How I work

Diagnose first. Price before parts.

I check airflow around a built-in first because tight cabinets make these units overheat, then the fans and controls.

Repair or replace?

Compressor-based coolers are worth repairing. Small thermoelectric units often are not, and I will say so before you pay for a visit.

Brands I work on

Sub-Zero, KitchenAid, U-Line, Whirlpool, Frigidaire, GE Monogram and most built-ins.
