What I see most

Common Miele problems and how I fix them

Miele appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Dishwasher: won't drain, F11 or drain fault

Usual cause: Drain pump or a blocked non-return valve.

What I do: Cleared or replaced. Common and repairable.

Dishwasher: intake fault or won't fill

Usual cause: Water inlet valve or the flow meter.

What I do: Diagnosed on site, part ordered if needed.

Washer: won't drain or won't spin

Usual cause: Drain pump, or the motor carbon brushes on older models.

What I do: Brushes and pumps are inexpensive on these machines.

Dryer: not heating (heat pump models take longer by design)

Usual cause: Heater, thermostat or a blocked filter path.

What I do: Cleaned and repaired, with honest expectations on heat-pump dryer times.

Oven: won't heat or fault code

Usual cause: Element, sensor or control.

What I do: Tested before ordering.
