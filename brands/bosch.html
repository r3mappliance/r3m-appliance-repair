What I see most

Common Bosch problems and how I fix them

Bosch appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

Dishwasher: E24 or E25 code, won't drain

Usual cause: Clogged or failed drain pump, a blocked check valve, or the drain hose.

What I do: I clear or replace the pump and check the whole drain path. One of the most common Bosch calls I get.

Dishwasher: E15, water in the base

Usual cause: Leak at a hose, pump seal or the sump. The float in the base pan trips and shuts the machine down.

What I do: I find the leak, fix it and reset the float. Not just tip it and send it.

Dishwasher: won't start, or lights on but nothing happens

Usual cause: Door latch, control module or a failed heat pump.

What I do: Diagnosed on site, quoted before any part is ordered.

Fridge: not cooling or frost inside

Usual cause: Fan, defrost system or door gasket.

What I do: Same-day in most cases.

Compact washer or dryer: won't drain, won't heat

Usual cause: Drain pump, door lock, heater or thermostat.

What I do: Common parts ordered quickly when not on the truck.

Bosch repair questions

My Bosch dishwasher shows E24. Can I fix it myself?

Sometimes it is just a clogged filter, and I will tell you that on the phone. If cleaning the filter does not clear it, the pump or check valve needs work.

Is a 10-year-old Bosch dishwasher worth repairing?

Yes. These machines commonly run 15 years or more. A pump or latch is a small fraction of a new 800 Series.

Are you a Bosch authorized servicer?

No. R3M is an independent repair company. If your Bosch is still under the manufacturer's warranty, use that first.
