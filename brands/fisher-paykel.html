What I see most

Common Fisher & Paykel problems and how I fix them

Fisher & Paykel appliances have their own patterns. These are the calls I get most often around Rockwall.

DishDrawer: F1 code or flood alarm

Usual cause: Water in the base from a lid seal, hose or pump leak. The flood switch trips.

What I do: I find the leak and fix it, then reset. Drying the base alone does not solve it.

DishDrawer: won't drain or won't fill

Usual cause: Drain pump, inlet valve or rotor.

What I do: Diagnosed and quoted on site.

Top-load washer: won't spin or out of balance

Usual cause: Suspension rods, or the motor and control on SmartDrive models.

What I do: Suspension is a quick fix. Motor issues are tested first.

Fridge: not cooling

Usual cause: Fan, defrost system or control.

What I do: Same-day for most.
